📚 Basics You Need to Learn
🔍
SEO — Keywords
Learn how people search for Smart Home products. Use Google Keyword Planner or Ubersuggest for free. Focus on keywords like "Best [product] under $100" or "[product] review 2025".
🔴 High Priority
✍️
Copywriting — Persuasive Writing
Learn how to write catchy headlines, powerful video Hooks, and compelling product descriptions. AIDA rule: Attention → Interest → Desire → Action.
🔴 High Priority
📊
Amazon Associates
Understand the dashboard, how to build affiliate links, usage rules (always disclose your commission), and how to improve Conversion rates.
🔴 High Priority
🎯
Paid Ads
Learn how to run ads on Google, TikTok, Pinterest, and YouTube. The core is understanding Targeting, Budget, and how to measure ROI. Don't start with ads before you have working organic content.
🟡 Important
🎬
Simple Video Production
You don't need an expensive camera — your phone is enough. Learn good lighting, clear audio, and editing with CapCut (free). Your first video doesn't have to be perfect.
🟡 Important
📌
Pinterest Strategy
Pinterest is a search engine, not social media! Good images + keywords = free traffic for years. Focus on Infographics and product comparisons.
🟡 Important
📝
Blog + SEO Content
Good articles bring traffic from Google for years. Learn article structure: H1, H2, Internal Links, and how to naturally add affiliate links.
🟡 Important
🎵
TikTok Algorithm
The Hook in the first 3 seconds determines everything. Use Trending Sounds, write Captions in English for the US market, and post 1-2 videos daily at first.
🟡 Important
📈
Analytics — Reading the Numbers
Learn to read YouTube Studio, TikTok Analytics, Pinterest Analytics, and Amazon Reports. Numbers tell you what works — not guesswork.
🟢 Learn Gradually
⚠️ Important Amazon Associates Rules
✅ You must add a Disclosure in every piece of content: "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases"
✅ Don't click your own links
✅ Don't share them in Emails
✅ You must make 3 sales in the first 180 days (you have until September!) — this is the most important thing now
✅ Smart Home commission rates range from 3%-8% depending on the category
